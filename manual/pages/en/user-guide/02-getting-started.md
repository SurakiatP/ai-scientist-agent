## What is getting started

AI Scientist works right away in the browser. You do not need to register or sign in. This page takes you from the first page to the chat screen, the main place to ask research questions, read answers and browse files.

{{shot:chat-home}}

The chat screen has two parts. The left bar has the {{ui:"New chat"}} button, the {{ui:"Projects"}} list, the {{ui:"Search chats"}} box and the menus {{ui:"My files"}}, {{ui:"Trash"}} and {{ui:"Settings"}}. The middle is the question box with buttons for the research mode and the research field.

## Good to know

- The left bar shows a connection status such as “Online · shared database”. See [[data-and-privacy]] for what it means
- The question box shows a faint sample question. Press the Tab key to put the sample question in the box, or click a sample card below it
- You can ask in Thai or English. Change the language of the screen in [[language-theme]]
- The system answers only scientific research questions. Read the limits in [[limits-and-safety]]
- Click {{ui:"Hide sidebar"}} to hide the left bar and make room for the work area

## Problems and fixes

- Problem: “Something went wrong”
  Fix: The page failed to render, but your chats stored in this browser are not affected. Click {{ui:"Try again"}}
- Problem: “Page not found”
  Fix: The link may be wrong or the page has moved. Click {{ui:"Go to chat"}}
