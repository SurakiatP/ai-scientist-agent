## What is changing the language and theme

The {{ui:"Settings"}} window lets you choose the language of the screen ({{ui:"ไทย"}} or English) and the colour theme ({{ui:"Light"}}, {{ui:"Dark"}} or {{ui:"System"}}, which follows the setting of your device). Use it to read the screen in another language, or to cut the glare in a dark room. The change applies at once.

## Good to know

- The language of the screen is separate from the language of your question. You can ask in Thai or English whichever language the screen uses
- A report document has its own {{ui:"Document language"}} choice. See [[create-report]]
- On the first page, before you enter the chat, the top bar also has buttons for the language and the theme
- This setting is stored in the browser you use, so set it again if you change computer or browser
